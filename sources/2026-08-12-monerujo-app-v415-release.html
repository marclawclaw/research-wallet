Monerujo v4.1.5 "Exolix"

Posted on Fri 01 November 2024 by m2049r in Updates #releases

This is major release v4.1.5 "Exolix" upgrading to Monero Core v0.18.3.4 and adding support for Exolix exchange

Warning: Uninstalling the app will delete your wallets on the device! Use the backup function to backup your wallets - you will need the CrAzYpass (aka "Restore Password") in order to open backed-up wallets - also in other apps.

Also available from Monerujo F-Droid Repo.

Changes since 4.0.8 (previous major release)

Upgrading to Monero Core v0.18.3.4 Add support for Exolix exchange Sticky Currency option Minor bugfixes Some refactoring
