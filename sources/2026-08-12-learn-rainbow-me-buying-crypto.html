    Buying Crypto with Rainbow

    At Rainbow, we believe everyone, everywhere should have access to cryptocurrencies and the underlying opportunities crypto provides, whether that’s buying digital collectibles or your ENS name, swapping tokens and providing liquidity, or trying new dapps and social networks.

    We have gone above and beyond to offer you the most flexibility and choice when it comes to how you convert your fiat to crypto, with more options than any other wallet—more providers, more payment options, more supported countries—plus higher limits, and fees starting at 0%.

    Read on to get all the details on how you can use Rainbow to quickly and easily go from fiat to crypto, all without ever leaving your wallet.

    Our fiat-to-crypto revamp has worldwide support

    Crypto is borderless and stateless, and it shouldn’t matter where you live in the world — you should be able to access it.

    We're expanding the horizons of what's possible by offering support for multiple currencies, including USD, EUR, GBP, NGN, and more.

    Use whatever payment method you like

    We know everyone’s preferences on how you like to pay are different—so we aim to support them all.

    Rainbow’s fiat-to-crypto offering supports on-ramping in almost every way you’d like to pay:

    • Apple Pay
    • ACH
    • Transfer from Coinbase, in Rainbow
    • Debit
    • Credit

    On-ramp into the network of your choice

    We believe Layer 2s are the future of crypto, and it should be seamless and easy to access Layer 2 networks.

    Depending on the provider you choose, you can on-ramp straight into any network Rainbow supports:

    • Ethereum mainnet
    • Optimism
    • Arbitrum
    • Polygon
    • BSC
    • Base

    As Rainbow expands to support more networks, we’ll let you on-ramp straight into those networks, too.
